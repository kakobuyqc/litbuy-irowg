02 / The research method

A Litbuy spreadsheet guide
built around useful decisions.

Use this Litbuy shopping guide to organize your research. This page offers category links and comparison advice rather than a downloadable sheet or verified platform inventory.

01.

Build a focused shortlist

Organize Litbuy product links by what you actually need. A shorter list makes duplicate listings and missing information easier to spot.

  • Keep sneaker finds separate from clothing finds.
  • Save the exact colorway and product variant.
  • Record the seller name and the date checked.
  • Remove dead product links from your active list.

02.

Read beyond the product title

For Litbuy clothing finds, measurements and fabric details are more useful than a familiar size label or a polished listing image.

  • Use a size chart and compare garment measurements.
  • Check material composition and care instructions.
  • Read seller reviews for recurring fit issues.
  • Use QC photos to inspect visible condition.

03.

Compare the complete purchase

A useful Litbuy finds list separates known costs from estimates so the cheapest listed item does not automatically become your first choice.

  • Note item price and the currency used.
  • Check domestic shipping and service fees.
  • Compare international shipping estimates.
  • Read the return policy before committing.

03 / Your research vocabulary

Find the details
that change the decision.

Use these shopping research terms to make your notes specific. Each group describes a different part of comparing a product, its fit and its likely total cost.

Product discovery

Sneaker finds and clothing finds help separate footwear from apparel research.

Group hoodie finds, T-shirt finds, jacket finds and pants finds by intended use.

Keep headwear finds and accessory finds in their own sections.

Fit and construction

A size chart, garment measurements and a shoe size guide support a more useful fit comparison.

Check material composition, fabric weight and care instructions where provided.

Save the exact colorway and product variant to avoid comparing different items.

Listing evidence

Compare product links, listing photos and available QC photos.

Read seller reviews alongside product descriptions and the stated return policy.

Record stock availability and a link check date; both can become outdated.

Budget and delivery

Start with item price, domestic shipping and any service fees.

Add international shipping, possible customs duties and applicable import taxes.

Note the exchange rate used and compare the estimated total cost.

04 / Clear answers

Before you
follow a find.

Quick answers about spreadsheet research, product evidence and the limits of this directory.

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

A Litbuy spreadsheet is a shopping research list used to organize product links and comparison notes. Useful fields include category, seller, listed price, measurements, available photos and the date each link was checked.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides a category directory and a shopping checklist. Its browsing links lead to CAFANS; it does not host a downloadable spreadsheet or a live Litbuy inventory feed.

How should I compare products from a shopping list?

Compare the exact product variant, measurements, materials, seller terms and available photos. Record item price separately from service fees, shipping and any applicable taxes or duties.

Can QC photos prove a branded item is authentic?

No. Quality-control photos can help show visible condition, color and measurements, but they do not prove authenticity. For branded purchases, seek verifiable provenance and an authorized or otherwise trustworthy seller.

Are prices, stock and delivery times guaranteed?

No. This guide does not verify live prices, stock or delivery estimates. Confirm current details on the destination listing and with the relevant provider before paying.
